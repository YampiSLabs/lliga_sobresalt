# Official Rules of the Lliga del Sobresalt

Welcome to the rules governing the monitoring of Catalonia's most eventful headlines. These rules have been drafted and approved by the **Intelligent Sobresalt Committee** to establish a completely satirical point system on the current media climate.

## 1. The Sobresalt Committee

The **Sobresalt Committee** is a completely fictional body, made up of alleged analysts, bar philosophers, and artificial intelligence algorithms with a great sense of humor.

This committee is solely responsible for analyzing in real time the front pages of local newspapers and assigning the corresponding scores based on the size of the headline, the degree of absurdity of the event, and its capacity to impact family WhatsApp groups.

## 2. Scoring System

Points accumulated by municipalities are calculated according to the **VAR (AI)** based on four major weighted coefficients:

- **Absurd Severity (40%)**: The level of surrealism of the incident (e.g., robberies with highly unlikely objects, unusual situations).
- **Social Alarm on Networks (30%)**: The number of times the headline can be indignantly shared on social networks.
- **Vandalism and Creativity (20%)**: The artistic capacity or improvisation displayed in the incident.
- **Classic Bonus (10%)**: An extra reserved for lifelong classics (e.g., late-night fights in nightlife areas).
